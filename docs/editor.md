# The editor

The main window is laid out like the Stream Deck app: the device at the top, the selected control's editor below it, and the actions on the right.

## Putting actions on controls

- **Drag** an action from the list onto a control. A control that already has one gets the new action; a Multi Action or workflow gets it added.
- **Drag a control onto another** to move its whole setup; the two swap when the other one is taken. Hold **Ctrl** to copy.
- **Drag a control off the device** to remove its action.
- **Double-click** a control to try it. The real action runs.

## Setting up a control

Click a control. Its editor opens below the device, and every change shows on the device right away; **Revert** puts back how the control was when you selected it.

**Light or dark:** DeckHost settings → Look → Theme (Dark, Light, or Same as the system).

**Undo and redo** (the arrows in the header, or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y; ⌘ on macOS) step back and forth through what you did to controls: drops, moves and swaps, removes and edits. Typing in a field counts as one step. Undo covers the controls of the device you're editing; profiles and pages are changed on their own pages.

- **Action**: its settings. Some Stream Deck plugins bring their own settings page, shown next to the editor.
- **Display**: text (font, size, color, alignment), an icon (from your icon packs or Iconify's free sets), an image or animated GIF, and the background: transparent, the action's colored tile, or a color.
- **Light** (controls with an LED): its color when pressed, and its idle effect.

## Icons

**Choose…** next to Icon opens the icon picker: your installed icon packs (Stream Deck `.streamDeckIconPack` files, or any zip or folder of images) and a search of Iconify's free icon sets. Single-color icons follow the text color.
