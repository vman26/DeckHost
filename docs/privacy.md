# Privacy

DeckHost has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. It only goes online for these:

| What | Where it connects | When |
|---|---|---|
| Update check | `api.github.com` (the releases repository) | Now and then, unless turned off in DeckHost settings |
| Downloading an update | `github.com` | When you choose to update |
| The plugin store | The store sources (DeckHost catalogs, OpenAction, the Elgato community archive), `raw.githubusercontent.com` for READMEs, icon hosts | When you open the store |
| Icon search | `api.iconify.design` | When you search for icons |
| Plugins | Wherever a plugin connects | Plugins run on their own; DeckHost doesn't see or limit their traffic |
| Web Deck | Nothing outside your network; phones connect to DeckHost | When you turn sharing on |

**Crash reports** stay on your computer. DeckHost offers to report one after a crash, by opening a GitHub issue in your browser that you can read and change first. **Support bundles** are files you save and share yourself.
