# DeckHost

**One platform. Unlimited control.**

DeckHost drives your control surfaces from one app: the MiraBox / StreamDock **N4 Pro**, **Elgato Stream Decks** (preview) and a **Web Deck** on your phone, tablet or a browser window. It runs on Windows, Linux and macOS, and it runs Stream Deck and OpenAction plugins.

- **Every control does what you put on it.** Drag an action onto a key, knob or touch zone. Give it text, an icon, a background and a light.
- **Profiles, pages and folders**, switched by hand, by a key, or by the app in front.
- **Multi Action, Key Logic and Workflows**: from a sequence of actions to a canvas of triggers, conditions, waits and loops.
- **Plugins**: DeckHost plugins, OpenAction plugins and Stream Deck plugins from the built-in store.
- **Updates** that are checked against their checksum before they install.
